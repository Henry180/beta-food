Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you order.
How do I pay for my order?
Build your meal, checkout with your name, phone and address, then pay by bank transfer to the Opay account shown at checkout. Tick "I have completed my payment" once you're done.
How long does delivery take?
Most orders arrive within 30–45 minutes depending on your location and how busy we are. We'll confirm your order on WhatsApp once payment is received.
Do I really get free water?
Yes! One free can of water is included whenever your order includes a main meal — just add it in the "Free Water" section of the menu.
Can I track my order?
After checkout you'll get a digital receipt you can save or send straight to our WhatsApp — that's also the fastest way to ask about your order status.
